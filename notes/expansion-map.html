    What this suggests

    At equal weights, including the retail-choice path, Texas (74), Michigan (73), and California (71) lead the 51 geographies. Read the score as a stack of percentiles. A high score means the public series line up. It is an outside view, with no Base install times, permit outcomes, or crew plan in it.

    The home-count factor rewards size. California has the most owner-occupied single-family homes, 6,771,188. With that weight set to zero, and every other factor including the launch path left on, the top three become Michigan (72), Texas (71), and California (68).

    Base's public footprint, marked on the map and kept out of the weights: Texas ranks 1 at equal weights (score 74), with SAIDI 225 minutes, retail delivery share 56.2%, and wholesale mix ERCOT 88%, MISO 5%, SPP 5%. Illinois ranks 9 (score 58), with SAIDI 144 minutes, retail delivery share 22.1%, and wholesale mix PJM 71%, MISO 29%. Georgia ranks 30 (score 47), with SAIDI 270 minutes, retail delivery share 0.0%, and wholesale mix No ISO balancing authority. Connecticut ranks 21 (score 53), with SAIDI 211 minutes, retail delivery share 15.8%, and wholesale mix ISO-NE 100%.

    Georgia's largest balancing authority in this file is Southern Company Services, Inc. - Trans. EIA reports no residential delivery service there. A utility contract, the shape of the reported Central Georgia EMC project, does not appear in the retail-choice factor, so Georgia's rank is a weak guide to that path.

    The longest state SAIDI in this file is West Virginia at 1013 minutes. The highest residential delivery share is Massachusetts at 61.5%. Commonwealth Edison reports 3,753,924 residential customers, SAIDI 77 minutes, and wholesale tag PJM. Central Georgia (Central Georgia El Member Corp) reports 62,546 residential customers and SAIDI 168 minutes.

    Install friction and a national price-volatility series sit outside the score. The Texas section is a short public day-ahead window, a congestion example rather than a yearly value of the nodes.

    From 2026-09-27 through 2026-10-28, KNAPP_RN in LZ_WEST (substation KNAPP) had a 95th-percentile day-ahead basis of $196.68/MWh across 768 hours, and 53% of those hours cleared at least $20/MWh above the zone. The median resource node in LZ_HOUSTON had a 95th-percentile basis of $6.36/MWh. The constraint with the most positive shadow-price rows was 6437__F.

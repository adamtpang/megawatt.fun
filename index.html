Tool 001 · fleet dispatch

The arbitrage floor is thin. That's the point.

A day-ahead dispatch planner for a fleet of home batteries, written in Go and run on real ERCOT day-ahead prices (hub average, captured 2026-08-13). It charges the midday solar trough, discharges the evening ramp, and refuses to cycle when the spread does not clear round-trip losses.

This is a floor, not a valuation. One cycle, energy-only, perfect foresight, no ancillary services, no retail hedge, no resilience value. On a mild August day the energy-only floor is under half a dollar per battery. The value of a fleet lives in the operating system around the arbitrage, which is exactly why that software is the interesting problem.

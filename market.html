Method, limits and sources
  • The score is an outside view from public data. It has no Base install times, permit outcomes, crew plans, financing terms or customer data.
  • The outage, price and bill layers are not in the score. Outages average 2024 and 2025 because one storm can swing a single year: Texas had 1,271 minutes in 2024 (Hurricane Beryl) and 224 in 2025.
  • Base footprint marks come from public posts and are not part of the score.
  • Not affiliated with Base Power.

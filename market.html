Method, limits and sources
  • The score is an outside view from public data. It has no Base install times, permit outcomes, crew plans, financing terms or customer data.
  • The outage, price and bill map layers are separate from the score. The outage layer averages 2024 and 2025 because one storm can swing a single year: Texas had 1,271 minutes in 2024 (Hurricane Beryl) and 224 in 2025. The score's own outage factors use the median of EIA-861 2021 to 2025, so a single storm year does not set a state's score.
  • Base footprint marks come from public, dated sources and are not part of the score. Operating means Base sells to homes there (Texas retail, Illinois ARES in ComEd). Announced means a utility program was announced. Equipment and installation (Colorado, Connecticut) is what Base's own site footer says it offers there: home battery equipment and installation, not a retail plan or a utility program.
  • Not affiliated with Base Power.

# Roadmap

**Program:** AI, energy, and chips operator learning loop  
**Window:** 2026-08-30 through 2026-11-28  
**Owner:** Adam  
**Working media vehicle:** pangaea.blog interview series  

## North star

One real AI, energy, battery, grid, or data-center operator uses Adam's research or software to validate, reject, or materially change a real decision.

The interview series is the learning and relationship engine. It is not measured first by views. Its first proof is better operator knowledge, one trusted relationship at a time, and one decision changed by evidence.

## Perfect version definition

The 90-day program is complete when all essential outcomes below are true:

1. Adam has completed at least five operator discovery interviews, with permission-aware notes and explicit recording or publication status.
2. At least three conversations have become approved public episodes or written interview artifacts on pangaea.blog.
3. optimism.fun contains one concise, sourced AI-power decision brief that corrects company-only crowding blind spots and records one external founder or operator decision.
4. megawatt.fun contains one narrow tool revision selected from repeated operator evidence, not from speculation.
5. One operator has tested the tool or brief against a real decision and the result, including rejection or no-change, is recorded honestly.
6. The guest ladder, research process, production checklist, consent state, and follow-up ritual are reusable for the next 90 days.

Essential excellence means thoughtful research, unusually good questions, reliable production, clear consent, and honest evidence. Large audience growth, sponsorship, a paid product, famous guests, and weekly publication are valuable later but are not required for this first proof.

### M1. Build and run the operator discovery ladder

**Status:** in progress  
**Target:** 2026-09-27  
**Outcome and user value:** Five conversations replace the current AI-power wedge assumptions with operator evidence.

Tasks in dependency order:

1. Build a 25-person private guest list in pokedex.life or a local research artifact. Score learning value, operating depth, accessibility, integrity, warmth of path, and relevance to the 5 to 50 MW AI-power problem.
2. Start with overlooked operators, engineers, integrators, researchers, and builders who have unusually specific knowledge but limited media attention.
3. Prepare a one-page research brief for each guest: biography, system they operate, strongest claims, counterevidence, vocabulary, recent work, and the one question only they can answer.
4. Adam manually invites the first ten candidates. Agents may draft and revise invitation copy but never send it.
5. Complete five interviews and ask every guest who understands the next layer of the problem better than they do.
6. Code findings into a constraint matrix: interconnection, power quality, workload flexibility, storage, backup generation, cooling, financing, chips, software, regulation, and staffing.

Completion criteria:

- Five completed interviews.
- Every interview has consent status, private notes, three corrected beliefs, one strongest quote if approved, one unresolved question, and one next-person referral.
- At least one constraint repeats independently across three interviews.

Focused work: 20 to 35 hours.  
Likely elapsed time: 2 to 4 weeks, driven by guest schedules.  
Confidence: medium.  
Dependencies and risks: response rate, scheduling latency, and guest consent. Famous guests are not required.

### M3. Build one operator-selected megawatt.fun tool

**Status:** not started  
**Target:** 2026-10-25  
**Outcome and user value:** One repeated operator decision becomes inspectable software.

Tasks in dependency order:

1. Freeze new feature ideas until M1 identifies a repeated constraint.
2. Ask one operator to state an acceptance test using sanitized or public inputs.
3. Choose the smallest tool that satisfies it. Candidate shapes include flexible workload scheduling, behind-the-meter battery sizing, transient load smoothing, time-to-power comparison, or value decomposition beyond energy-only arbitrage.
4. Implement the narrow model with explicit assumptions, fixtures, tests, and failure states.
5. Review the result with the operator before broadening the scope.

Completion criteria:

- The feature traces to at least three interviews and one named acceptance test.
- Tests pass and assumptions are inspectable.
- The operator says reuse, revise, reject, or irrelevant. Silence is not validation.

Focused engineering: 25 to 50 hours.  
Likely elapsed time: 2 to 4 weeks.  
Confidence: low until M1 selects the workflow.  
Dependencies and risks: access to representative data, feature creep, and mistaking enthusiasm for workflow value.

## ETA model

Assumptions:

- Adam contributes 8 to 12 focused hours weekly.
- Agents prepare research, drafts, analysis, and reversible local artifacts.
- Adam performs invitations, conversations, consent, account setup, publication, and all external communication.
- Five interviews require ten or more invitations and two to four weeks of calendar latency.
- One narrow software revision takes less time than validating the right revision.

Time to useful: 2 to 4 weeks, after the first three interviews reveal repeated constraints.  
Time to excellent: 8 to 12 weeks, after a decision brief, tool revision, and operator pilot.  
Time to perfect for this roadmap: 90 days, assuming at least one external operator participates.  

## Risks and decision gates

- **Trademark:** Do not use Pokédex, Pokedex, Pokémon names, characters, visual trade dress, or confusingly similar branding for the public show. The existing pokedex.life domain and Pokémon-styled product also merit a separate rebrand or trademark-lawyer review before public promotion or commercialization. Keeping a guest list private does not clear a publicly exposed product name. Obtain a trademark lawyer's clearance before investing materially in any final show or product name.
- **Recording consent:** Recording and transcription laws vary by jurisdiction. Obtain explicit written consent before recording or transcribing and separate approval before publication.
- **Guest trust:** Research deeply, do not surprise guests with adversarial framing, provide factual corrections, and distinguish private background from publishable material.
- **Private information:** Do not place private contact details, unapproved transcripts, personal relationship scores, or unpublished operator data in public repositories.
- **Feature drift:** No megawatt.fun feature begins without repeated interview evidence and an operator acceptance test.
- **False market proof:** A compliment, episode view, social follow, or famous guest is not evidence that a product solves an operator problem.
- **Concentration:** Base Power and Casey Handmer are valuable targets, not gates. The program succeeds first by learning from overlooked operators with direct experience.
- **External communication:** Codex may draft invitations and follow-ups, but Adam sends every message and performs every call.

- Candidates researched

Change milestone status only when its completion criteria are met. Record negative results without softening them. Recalculate downstream timing when interview access or the selected operator workflow changes.

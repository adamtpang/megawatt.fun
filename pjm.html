    Policy risk to watch

    Nonbypassable transmission takes the transmission tag off the table.

    This is already the rule in Ohio: AEP Ohio, FirstEnergy Ohio and AES Ohio recover transmission from every customer through a nonbypassable rider, so the page counts their transmission tags as 0. PPL Electric has proposed the same in Pennsylvania: transmission (NITS) as a charge every customer pays the utility, whatever their supplier. A supplier could then no longer save its members money by cutting their transmission tags. Base asked the Pennsylvania PUC to let suppliers opt out and take on those costs themselves (dockets P-2026-3065185, P-2026-3065186 and P-2024-3047290). Tick “Model PPL’s nonbypassable transmission proposal” above to see what the PPL zone is worth without it.

To stretch it

  • Set the thermostat to 80°F or turn the AC off.
  • Skip the oven, dryer, and pool pump.
  • Keep the fridge closed.

Running power for each appliance is an assumption.

How it works

  1. Your battery charges when power is cheap, usually at night.
  2. It powers your home when power is expensive, and can help the grid when demand spikes.
  3. It always keeps a backup reserve for outages.

What's real and what's assumed

  • Real: hourly ERCOT day-ahead prices for Houston Hub, cached with source and download time.
  • Real: battery size, 39.2 kWh, the smaller Base Core (pv magazine USA).
  • Assumed: 5 kW charge rate, 88% round trip, half the battery kept as backup, the shape of a day's home use, and appliance power.
  • Simulated: what the battery did. This is not Base's real control logic, fleet behavior, or revenue.

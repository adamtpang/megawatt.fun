Score assumptions

Each active factor uses min–max scaling among complete, eligible scenarios; ties receive 50%. Missing an active factor prevents scoring; missing a zero-weight factor does not. Eligibility and a known price series are always required. Scores are relative priorities, not probabilities or ROI. Feeder spare capacity and area are screening proxies; export permission, setbacks, network constraints and site-specific dispatch are not modeled. Energy uses the shared battery settings above.

What this shows

More crews. Then what?

A queueing model of eligible jobs moving through six stages. It tests which capacity change increases completed installations. It is not Base’s operating system, forecast or facility layout.

All cycle times, staffing, buffers, variability and interruptions are synthetic. One shift is 8 production hours. The model starts after a 2-shift warm-up. The forecast uses a fresh seeded run, warms the pipeline, then measures 30 shifts. Capacity mode assumes a replenished supply of jobs and batteries.

Production time pauses between shifts; this is not calendar lead time. Travel routes, permitting delays, customer availability, shared crews, actual material shortages and rework are not modeled. No Base operational or customer data is used. The red outline follows the forecast constraint; live bars show recent stage states.

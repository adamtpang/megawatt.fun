K = 0.727

Humanity's position on the Kardashev scale, computed from the latest real world energy figure, with the formula and every assumption on the table.

The number

18.78 terawatts, continuously

World primary energy consumption in 2024 was 592.2 EJ (Energy Institute Statistical Review of World Energy 2025). Spread over the year that is an average power draw of 18.78 TW. Sagan's interpolation puts that at K = 0.727.

K = (log₁₀(P watts) − 6) / 10  =  (log₁₀(1.878 × 10¹³) − 6) / 10  =  0.727

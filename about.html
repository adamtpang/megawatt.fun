About megawatt.fun

megawatt.fun is an independent energy tooling suite built in public by Adam Pangelinan. Its calculations, source material, code, and known limitations are published for inspection.

Purpose

Useful models with the caveats attached

megawatt.fun turns public energy data into small, inspectable tools and studies. The current suite includes a tested Go battery-dispatch planner, a live view of ERCOT settlement prices, a transparent Kardashev-scale calculator, and a sourced study of energy companies.

The dispatch model intentionally covers one daily cycle, energy-only arbitrage, and perfect day-ahead price knowledge. The homepage and public README explain what the model omits, including ancillary services, real-time forecasting, degradation, retail-plan interactions, and resilience value.

Independence

No company affiliation implied

megawatt.fun is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or built from non-public information belonging to ERCOT, Base Power, or any other battery company. Company names and public data sources appear only where they are relevant to the educational tools or cited research.

The live ERCOT page is informational and simplified. Anyone settling money or making an operational decision should use ERCOT’s own systems and the appropriate professional process, not this independent project.
